THE NAME BEHIND THE KITCHEN

A spark of brilliance.
A bowl of comfort.

Dhyutidhar is our founder’s chosen Shiva-inspired name, with “Lord of brilliance” as its inspiration.

Our idea is simple: bring the comfort of an Telangana meal to a busy Hyderabad day. Familiar ingredients, satisfying portions and a focused menu for lunch and dinner.
